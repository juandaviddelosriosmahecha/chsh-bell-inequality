\title{Beyond CHSH: Experimental Correlator-Level Analysis of Quantum
Randomness under Superconducting Hardware Noise}

\author{Juan David de los Rios Mahecha}

\begin{abstract}
We ask what statistical information is discarded when a Bell experiment is
reduced to the single CHSH parameter $S$. Using full joint distributions
$P(a,b|x,y)$, per-correlator analysis, and explicit noise and trust models on
superconducting hardware, we test whether complete statistics add experimental
or cryptographic information beyond $S$. A null result is treated as a valid
outcome.
\end{abstract}
